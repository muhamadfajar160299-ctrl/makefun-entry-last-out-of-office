## Entry

> I'm out of office — I quit to become a barista. Your email is queued behind fourteen flat whites and will be answered by the steam wand, shortly, with a hiss. Please do not reply-all to the coffee.

## How it works

`main.py` (standard library only) reads `entry.txt` shipped in this repository
and copies it verbatim to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` with mode `0644`.
